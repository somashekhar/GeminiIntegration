Summary:
• Artificial intelligence has evolved from early symbolic logic into GPU-accelerated deep learning, transformer architectures, and generative systems, with leading firms targeting Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).
• Modern AI capabilities center on machine learning, probabilistic decision-making, and specialized reasoning models, though data-driven systems still face challenges with accurate recall and hallucinations.
• The recent explosion of generative AI applications has introduced significant business and societal friction, driving urgent debates over deepfake scandals, environmental impacts, safety risks, and regulatory oversight.

Insight:
The technological shift from explicit, rule-based symbolic systems to probabilistic deep learning has greatly accelerated commercial utility while simultaneously shifting the industry's critical bottleneck from technical capability to ethical governance and operational reliability.
